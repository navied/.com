Local Raccoon Reportedly "Just Vibing" In Recycling Bin

Witnesses described the animal as calm, focused, and apparently unfazed by the growing media attention.

By Staff Writer · Updated 11:40 PM · 4 min read

In a development that has captivated no one of consequence, a neighborhood raccoon was spotted reclining in a blue recycling bin, described by witnesses as "unbothered."

"He looked at me like I was the one trespassing," said one resident. Authorities have declined to intervene, citing the animal's extremely relatable energy.
